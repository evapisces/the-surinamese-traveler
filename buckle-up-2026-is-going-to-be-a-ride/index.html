Buckle Up: 2026 Is Going to Be a Ride 🌍✈️

Eva Mans

01 Mar 2026 — 1 min read

If 2025 taught me anything, it's that life is too short not to fill it with incredible experiences. From dancing (okay, screaming) in the crowd at a BLACKPINK concert in Chicago, to watching the sun dip below the horizon on a cruise in Aruba — last year was genuinely one for the books. So many memories made with the people who matter most, and honestly? I wouldn't change a single thing.

But instead of riding that high and coasting, the plan is simple: keep it going.

2026 is already off to a strong start. I rang in the New Year in DC with one of my favorite people, and followed it up with a beautiful trip to the Adirondacks to take in some ski competitions. (Blogs on both are coming soon, so stay tuned! 👀)

So… what's next?

I'm so glad you asked. 😄

This year is shaping up to be the most travel-packed one yet. The crown jewel of the year is an epic women-only trip to Japan in October — something I have been looking forward to for a long time, and I cannot wait to share every moment of it here. Beyond that, the goal is to keep the momentum going with monthly weekend or week-long trips to various spots around the US. We're talking first-ever trips to Texas in April and Michigan in May, with more to be added to the list as the year unfolds.

Basically, if you thought 2025 was fun to follow along — buckle up, because 2026 is going to be a bumpy, beautiful, absolutely epic ride. 🎉

I'll see you out there. 🌍
